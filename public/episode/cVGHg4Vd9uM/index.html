Marina Mogilko interviews seven AI founders, CEOs, and researchers to identify the biggest untapped opportunities in AI. The core insight is that while 76% of small business owners use AI, only 14% have integrated it into core operations—revealing a massive gap between AI awareness and actual implementation. Rather than chasing oversaturated AI markets, the guests recommend targeting "boring" industries with inefficient workflows, such as property management where customers experience multi-day delays between inquiry and paperwork. The episode reveals that domain knowledge—the tacit understanding of an industry's pain points—is becoming the primary competitive advantage, since building AI solutions is now cheap and fast. Speakers including Amjad Masad (Replit CEO) and Andrew Ng (Coursera founder) emphasize that deciding what to build matters more than technical execution, and that existing industry experience can be transformed into a valuable business opportunity.

Key Takeaways

  • The real AI opportunity gap exists between awareness and integration—76% of small businesses use AI tools but only 14% have built it into core operations, creating a massive addressable market in automation of existing workflows
  • Domain knowledge is now the primary competitive advantage—your tacit understanding of an industry's pain points, customer delays, and exception cases that never made it online is more valuable than coding ability since prototyping costs have plummeted
  • Look for money bleeding between steps in traditional workflows—property management loses revenue with each day of delay between listing view, reply, viewing, paperwork, and deposit, a pattern that repeats across all industries still using phones, email, and spreadsheets
  • The bottleneck has shifted from building to deciding what to build—with AI making development cheap and fast, product management judgment and customer conversation skills now matter more than engineering expertise for identifying viable opportunities
  • Test your side hustle systematically over 90 days with a five-step plan—your current job is your research lab; look for the recurring problem you solve weekly, then build an AI solution and validate with paying customers before scaling
