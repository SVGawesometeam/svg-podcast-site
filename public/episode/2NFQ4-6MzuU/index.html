In this episode of the Silicon Valley Girl Podcast, Marina Mogilko shares This episode is not an interview with a guest. Instead, Marina Mogilko presents her own research and strategy on Generative Engine Optimization (GEO), explaining how Silicon Valley Girl podcast increased its AI visibility from non-existent to 5th most visible among 11 tracked podcast brands in just two months. Marina demonstrates that 35% of US consumers now start shopping inside AI assistants rather than search engines, with conversion rates from ChatGPT recommendations reaching 15.9% compared to 1.76% from organic Google traffic. She walks through five specific steps her team implemented to make the podcast discoverable to AI crawlers: diagnosing AI visibility issues, building a machine-readable podcast website with full transcripts, fixing structured data identity markers, optimizing for specific AI queries, and tracking measurable results across multiple AI platforms.

Key Takeaways

  • AI invisibility is now a critical business threat—35% of US shoppers start with AI assistants, and if you're not in the AI's top 3-5 recommendations, the sale ends before customers reach you
  • Most websites appear fine to humans but are invisible to AI crawlers because their HTML lacks required parameters and transcripts are hidden behind JavaScript—audit your site immediately by asking Claude about your AI visibility
  • Building a machine-readable website requires static HTML pages that load fully before any interaction, with complete transcripts in plain text; this single change added 8,000-15,000 words per episode that AI can now read and attribute to your brand
  • Structured data and identity markers in databases like Schema.org and Wikipedia determine how AI models categorize and understand your business—getting these wrong means AI won't know what you do even if it reads your content
  • First-mover advantage exists now: 92% of marketers plan to optimize for AI search but only 40% are actually doing it, meaning the gap between intent and action is your window to capture visibility before competition intensifies
