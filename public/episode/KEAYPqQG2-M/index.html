In this episode of the Silicon Valley Girl Podcast, Marina Mogilko interviews Anton Osika, Co-Founder of Lovable, a $13B AI company. Anton Osika, co-founder of Lovable, discusses how the no-code AI platform has evolved from "vibe coding" demos to powering real businesses. Lovable-built apps now receive nearly a billion visits monthly, with over a third of users already generating revenue. Anton explains the framework for turning personal tools into viable businesses: break problems into testable pieces, talk to 10 people before building, and start with paid products rather than free offerings. He emphasizes that as building becomes easier, creativity and problem-solving become the true differentiators. Throughout the episode, Anton and Marina redesign her website live and explore how to set up backends without coding, demonstrating Lovable's practical capabilities for founders without technical backgrounds.

Key Takeaways

  • Vibe coding is dead—Lovable users are now running actual businesses on the platform, with some generating $300,000+ monthly revenue, requiring serious infrastructure and reliability, not demos
  • The best entrepreneurs to emerge now are knowledge workers with 11+ years of professional experience who understand specific problems deeply and can now solve them without waiting for developers
  • Break problems into small, testable pieces before building; iterate toward perfection rather than over-engineering from the start, and always talk to 10 people before you build
  • Charge from day one—your first product shouldn't be free; pricing validates that you're solving a real problem and helps you understand customer value perception
  • Creativity is the biggest differentiator in AI entrepreneurship right now; everyone in knowledge work should build personal agents for themselves to understand how they work and gain competitive advantage
