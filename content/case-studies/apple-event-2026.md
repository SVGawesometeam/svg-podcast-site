# Apple Event 2026: inside the keynote, on every platform

**Partner:** Apple · **Format:** event coverage · **Channels:** Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, X, newsletter

## The brief

Bring a first-hand point of view from inside the Apple Event to a global community interested in technology, entrepreneurship, AI and the future of work, fast and in the native format of each platform.

## What we published

22 original posts over the event days: real-time updates, short-form video, visual explainers and a personal behind-the-scenes story, plus a dedicated newsletter feature.

## Results

- **30.2M+** post-level views across the campaign
- **26M** views on Instagram Reels, the top Reel reaching **16.3M**
- **2.25M** views on TikTok across three posts
- **730K** on Instagram carousels, **459K** on YouTube Shorts, **454K** on X
- Newsletter feature delivered to **20,511** subscribers: **49.27%** open rate, 10,105 unique opens, 481 clicks

## Why it worked

A moment the audience cares about, covered by someone they already trust, in the format each platform rewards. The audience responded within hours and shared the content widely.
