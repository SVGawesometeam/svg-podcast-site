# Hub drafts: what to check before approving

Written 2026-10-09. All ten drafts were produced from the episode data (titles, summaries,
takeaways, transcripts) and every quoted sentence was matched word for word against the transcript
of the episode it is attributed to. What the drafts cannot guarantee, and a person should check:

1. **Quote times.** The transcripts carry no timestamps, so each quote's time is the chapter that
   matches its content, with its position in the transcript as a sanity check. Most agree; these
   were chosen on the chapter title where the position estimate pointed to the neighbouring chapter,
   so check them first: Allie Miller 07:14 (ai-tools-and-workflows), Robby Stein 14:07 and Cassey Ho
   31:36 (creator-economy-and-personal-brand), Blake Scholl 20:33 (leadership-and-mindset), Alex
   15:11 (money-and-investing).
2. **Voice.** Each take is written in first person as Marina. Change anything she would not say.
3. **Numbers from summaries rather than transcript lines:** "71% misjudge their level" and "25% to
   70% skills change" (ai-skills-and-careers); Slow Ventures' $60M fund and the $1.7M round
   (creator-economy-and-personal-brand); "run my own companies for 14 years" (leadership-and-mindset);
   Jurvetson relaying Jack Clark's "30% chance by next year" (ai-safety-and-the-future).
4. **Unlabelled transcript turns** were never quoted, only paraphrased: Blake Scholl's "dark matter"
   line, Howie Liu on agents not running companies, Anne Wojcicki's chest-CT lines, Aravind
   Srinivas's "go deep one to two years" (attributed through Marina's solo episode instead).
5. **Transcript problems found on the way, for the content pass:** speaker labels are swapped in
   parts of the Esther Wojcicki (qNt5TNn9D9Y, around 29:29) and Max Tegmark (KF_uNAxPFFA)
   transcripts; "Prenuvo" is transcribed "pernuvo"/"pranuvo" in Anne Wojcicki's episode; the Gary
   Vaynerchuk (4vIIeCqHYXA) and Luis von Ahn (GDeEATJcbJo) episodes have no summary or takeaways.
6. **Health and money** drafts are written as "what the guests said", not advice; keep it that way.
   The health draft says in Marina's voice "I have familial hypercholesterolemia": the Anne Wojcicki
   episode summary states it, and it is already on that episode page, but a personal health
   statement on a hub needs Marina's own yes. Cut it if in doubt.
7. Two hubs (leadership, creator economy) each quote the same Oscar Hoglund episode; fine, noted.

Approval: say which files are approved and I move each to `content/topics/<slug>.md`, which
publishes it on the hub at the next build. Edits can be made in the draft file directly.
