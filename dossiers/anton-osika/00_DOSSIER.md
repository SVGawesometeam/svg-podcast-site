# ANTON OSIKA — SVG GUEST DOSSIER
_Prepared 8 September 2026. Built from 5 full timestamped interview transcripts (Sep 2025 – Apr 2026), 44 X posts and 32 LinkedIn posts from the last 90 days, funding filings and press._

**RESEARCH**

**WHO HE IS**

Anton Osika is co-founder and CEO of Lovable, a Stockholm platform where you describe an app in plain English and the AI builds it, hosts it, and connects payments, email and a database. No code is written by the user. It sells on subscription: free plan, $25 Pro, $50 Business, and enterprise contracts, and it makes money on the building, not the hosting, which is free ([Osika on X, 31 Aug 2026](https://x.com/antonosika/status/2094369130094957038); [TWiST 28:20](https://youtu.be/ST4UwQodhDs&t=1700s)). He runs the company with co-founder and CTO Fabian Hedin. He is 36 (born 10 August 1990) and became a billionaire in December 2025 when the company hit a $6.6B valuation ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anton_Osika)). The current hook: on 12 August 2026 Lovable raised **$400M at a $13.3B valuation**, doubling its price in eight months ([TechCrunch, 12 Aug 2026](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/12/lovable-confirms-new-13-3b-valuation-raises-another-400m/)).

**PATH**

He studied physics — BSc at Hong Kong University of Science and Technology, MSc in Engineering Physics at KTH in Stockholm — and worked on the ATLAS experiment at CERN. He left, and his reason is worth hearing: "they're all working on these impossible problems without any elasticity" ([Sequoia 5:31](https://youtu.be/TqAgejIX7uU&t=331s), VERBATIM). He was an early engineer at Sana Labs, then co-founder and CTO of Depict.ai, a Y Combinator e-commerce recommendations company that raised $20M and then lost momentum ([Sequoia 6:46](https://youtu.be/TqAgejIX7uU&t=406s); [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anton_Osika)).

The founding insight came from being the person everyone asked for a favour. "All my life I've been approached by people who wanted to find a great engineer to help them realize their dreams or the ideas… 0.5% of the world can even code" ([Sequoia 7:21](https://youtu.be/TqAgejIX7uU&t=441s), VERBATIM). His decision was to aim at the other side: "let's build something for the 99% and not this productivity boost for developers" ([Sequoia 7:36](https://youtu.be/TqAgejIX7uU&t=456s), VERBATIM). In 2023 he released GPT Engineer as open source, woke up one morning convinced, biked to Fabian Hedin's place and called him for a walk ([GAEA 3:36](https://youtu.be/xveOgrL5UC4&t=216s)). They launched the commercial product as Lovable in **November/December 2024 with eight people** ([Shira Lazar 9:44](https://youtu.be/jr9ESVOtyMo&t=584s)).

The August 2026 round: $400M at $13.3B, led by Menlo Ventures with the Scaleup Europe Fund (managed by EQT) co-leading. In the eight months since the December Series B, projects built on Lovable went from 25M to 60M, monthly visits to Lovable-built apps went from 200M to 900M, and ARR nearly tripled. Annualised run rate passed **$500M in June 2026**. **HIS THESIS / WHAT HE SAYS PUBLICLY**

**The 99% argument is the whole company.** Fewer than 1% of people could ever build software, and he thinks that's where most good ideas were stranded: "99% of the good ideas are in the 99% who were not able to before" ([Masters of Scale 5:53](https://youtu.be/9FGMhz-e97k&t=353s), VERBATIM). He frames Lovable as a technical co-founder you rent for $25 a month.

**On jobs, he refuses the framing rather than softening it.** "There's this 'oh AI is going to take jobs' mindset for some people. I think it's the wrong perspective of a fixed pie mindset. And we're now baking entirely new pies that you can take a slice of" ([Shira Lazar 25:36](https://youtu.be/jr9ESVOtyMo&t=1536s), VERBATIM). Asked directly whether vibe coding is cheating, he compared it to the calculator: once considered cheating, now just a tool "to increase your competitiveness in the job market" ([TWiST 49:53](https://youtu.be/ST4UwQodhDs&t=2993s), VERBATIM).

**He has a dated prediction on the record.** In October 2025 he said that within five years, all new software will be built by explaining it in plain English, and "there will be handwritten code in the same sense that there's like handmade clothes" ([Masters of Scale 17:02](https://youtu.be/9FGMhz-e97k&t=1022s), VERBATIM). In September 2025 he predicted that within 18 months — so by roughly March 2027 — a Lovable-built app would be twice as secure on average as one built from scratch by a competent human ([TWiST 23:52](https://youtu.be/ST4UwQodhDs&t=1432s)).

**Where he says the advantage now sits.** "Creativity is becoming the only moat in building great products" ([X, 7 Sep 2026](https://x.com/antonosika/status/2096899931240112440), VERBATIM), and separately, "Community is one of the remaining moats in technology that can't be replicated" ([X, 31 Jul 2026](https://x.com/antonosika/status/2083241061821157460), VERBATIM). His stated bottleneck for a good product is not technical: "the biggest bottlenecks for building good software products is some kind of product judgment and just empathy for what your customers want" ([Masters of Scale 14:29](https://youtu.be/9FGMhz-e97k&t=869s), VERBATIM).

**He names who loses.** "Some SaaS companies that are relying on hefty margins today are going to naturally see a decline in the revenue" as single-person startups with better product taste undercut them ([Masters of Scale 10:31](https://youtu.be/9FGMhz-e97k&t=631s), VERBATIM). He wants Lovable to be where the first one-person unicorn gets built ([Masters of Scale 7:24](https://youtu.be/9FGMhz-e97k&t=444s)), and he says publicly the plan is the first trillion-dollar company out of Europe ([TWiST 18:09](https://youtu.be/ST4UwQodhDs&t=1089s)).

**QUICK EXPLAINER — WHAT "VIBE CODING" ACTUALLY MEANS**

The term was coined by AI researcher Andrej Karpathy, not by Osika, and Osika says so plainly ([Shira Lazar 16:31](https://youtu.be/jr9ESVOtyMo&t=991s)). It means you never look at the code: you describe what you want in plain language, look at the working app, and keep talking to the AI until it's right. Osika's framing on air: humans used to read the ones and zeros the computer reads, then stopped; this is the next step in that same shift ([Masters of Scale 3:19](https://youtu.be/9FGMhz-e97k&t=199s)). Lovable's specific bet is an opinionated stack — one fixed set of technical choices under the hood — which he argues makes it more reliable for non-engineers than open-ended tools ([TWiST 26:10](https://youtu.be/ST4UwQodhDs&t=1570s)).

**WHY IT'S HAPPENING NOW**

Three things converged. The models got good enough to hold a whole product in context and act on it, and the big labs ship a materially better one roughly every three months ([TWiST 25:59](https://youtu.be/ST4UwQodhDs&t=1559s)). The plumbing got absorbed: payments, database, email, custom domain and hosting are now one-click inside the platform, so the non-technical builder never touches infrastructure ([Shira Lazar 15:10](https://youtu.be/jr9ESVOtyMo&t=910s)). And the audience turned out not to be hobbyists — corporate product managers, designers, finance and ops teams found it first, because building the thing is faster than writing the document asking for it. Meta's internal mantra, as Osika reports it: "demo, don't memo" ([Masters of Scale 9:07](https://youtu.be/9FGMhz-e97k&t=547s), VERBATIM).

**DEFENDED POSITIONS & PUSHBACK FILE**

**1. "In 18 months, AI-built software will be 2x more secure than what a good human builds."** Said September 2025 ([TWiST 23:52](https://youtu.be/ST4UwQodhDs&t=1432s)). **Counter:** studies put the share of AI-generated code containing security vulnerabilities at roughly 40–62%, and the Cloud Security Alliance counted at least 20 documented incidents across AI-built apps in a single research pass, nearly all tracing to missing authorization and secrets exposed in client code ([Superblocks analysis](https://www.superblocks.com/blog/lovable-vulnerabilities)). CVE-2025-48757, affecting 170+ Lovable-built apps, exposed home addresses, API keys and personal debt figures because generated projects shipped without row-level security ([VibeAppScanner](https://vibeappscanner.com/lovable-security)). **His documented defence:** he does not claim parity today — "I cannot say that it's more secure than a security expert today… I do recommend looping in a security expert for life and death applications" ([TWiST 22:43](https://youtu.be/ST4UwQodhDs&t=1363s), VERBATIM) — and he argues the direction is inevitable, comparing it to the 1960s decision to let a computer, not a pilot, fly the rocket ([TWiST 21:55](https://youtu.be/ST4UwQodhDs&t=1315s)). The AIUC-1 certification in July 2026 and "secured code by default" as a stated priority in the funding announcement are the concrete response.

**2. "You can build a real business on this, not just a prototype."** **Counter:** independent comparisons of the 2026 tools put Lovable at roughly 7/10 on production quality, behind v0 and Windsurf, and a common recommendation is to prototype on Lovable and then hire a developer to ship ([tool comparison round-up](https://www.vibecodingacademy.ai/blog/lovable-vs-bolt-vs-replit-comparison-2026)). Replit raised $400M at $9B in the same window, so the category is well funded and contested ([Tech Insider](https://tech-insider.org/replit-vs-lovable-vs-bolt-new-2026/)). **His documented defence:** he points at 900M monthly visits to apps built on the platform as proof it is production traffic, not toys — "that's not like 'oh someone vibe coded something cool.' This is real production software" ([Shira Lazar 29:31](https://youtu.be/jr9ESVOtyMo&t=1771s), VERBATIM) — and concedes the boundary himself: Facebook-scale is out of reach, the target is 95% of SaaS ([TWiST 48:43](https://youtu.be/ST4UwQodhDs&t=2923s)).

**3. "Working in person, at very high intensity, is how you build this."** He requires in-office work ([X, 19 Jun 2026](https://x.com/antonosika/status/2067985818954948732)) and says many at Lovable work 996-plus. **Counter:** he acknowledges losing senior candidates over it and answers "but that's maybe good" ([Sequoia 43:17](https://youtu.be/TqAgejIX7uU&t=2597s)). The tension is that a company whose entire pitch is that one person can now do what a team did is itself running on 250 people at maximum intensity. **His documented defence:** he frames it as explicit expectation-setting rather than imposition, says people with families are accommodated if impact is clear, and separately argues that hiring humans still beats agents because "ultra-reliable long-horizon is very valuable" ([LinkedIn, 28 Jun 2026](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/antonosika_we-have-a-great-team-and-were-hiring-because-activity-7476996877348040705-fAuj), VERBATIM).

**The one incident with context:** on 3 March 2026 a security researcher reported through Lovable's bug bounty that a flaw in its API let anyone with a free account read other users' project source code, database credentials and AI chat history. Lovable patched it for newly created projects but left every project created before November 2025 exposed. The researcher disclosed publicly on 20 April 2026, 48 days later. Exposed material included records from a Danish nonprofit and personal details tied to employees at several large tech companies. Lovable initially called the exposed data intentional behaviour and pointed at unclear documentation and its bug bounty partner, then issued a partial apology acknowledging that "pointing to documentation issues alone was not enough" ([TNW](https://thenextweb.com/news/lovable-vibe-coding-security-crisis-exposed); [Vibe Graveyard timeline](https://vibegraveyard.ai/story/lovable-bola-api-48-days-exposed/)). No public statement from Osika himself has been found on this specific incident. His general position, stated repeatedly since, is that security is the platform's job, not the user's: "you can build and run your business… without needing to become a security expert" ([X, 12 Aug 2026](https://x.com/antonosika/status/2087479638939652601), VERBATIM).
