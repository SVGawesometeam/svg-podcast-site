This week, everyone at Lovable got a small piece of #Sweden for their desk. 🇸🇪. 

The Dala horse has been hand-painted in the Dalarna region for centuries — a symbol of Swedish craftsmanship that’s sat in homes, offices, and shops across the country for generations. Now every new joiner at Lovable gets one with a nameplate that’s genuinely theirs, made by hand just a few hundred kilometers from where Lovable started.

Employees pick from 25+ horses (in Lovable colors), add their name + location, drop in the logo — and the design goes straight to artisans in Dalarna, who hand-paint it and ship it to their desk.

The best part: the entire ordering experience was built in days by a non‑engineer on the People Team. (Shoutout to Liv Törnberg).  No spec doc. No procurement marathon. Just someone who understood the problem and built a custom solution end-to-end on Lovable.
