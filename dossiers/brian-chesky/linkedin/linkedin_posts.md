Today, we welcomed Pepijn Rijvers as Airbnb’s new Chief Business Officer, as Dave Stephenson steps back to take a much-deserved beat.

Pepijn joins us from Tripadvisor Group, where he was Chief Business Officer, and before that spent 13 years at Booking.com. 

Pepijn's experience at Booking gave him a clear view of why Airbnb needs to follow a very different path. Traditional travel platforms are largely built around intent: you know where you’re going, and they help you book it. Pepijn believes Airbnb has the opportunity to start much earlier, helping people discover where to go, where to stay, and what to do once they get there—to help people discover what makes a place special. 

I want to thank Dave for everything he’s done for Airbnb. Since he joined in 2018, he’s served as CFO, CBO, and Head of Employee Experience—three of the hardest jobs in the company, two of them at the same time. Airbnb is stronger today than it’s ever been, not because of any single quarter, but because of years of work compounding. Dave’s leadership is a big part of that.

We’ve rebuilt Airbnb to be an AI-native company. We're shipping nearly 80% more features and improvements this year compared to last, moving from idea to launch as much as 60% faster.

This isn’t a one-quarter story. We’re executing at a much higher level than we were just a few years ago, and I think that’s one of the reasons we're growing faster than our largest peers.

The long-term opportunity is bigger than ever.
